A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Painted with
feeling.

I'm Rehana, an oil painter drawn to places and moments that evoke something difficult to put into words.

My paintings are shaped by atmosphere, memory, and the quiet beauty of the everyday. I love the way light can transform a familiar scene, and how a simple place can hold an entire story.

Each piece is painted by hand, layer by layer, with the hope that it will mean something a little different to everyone who encounters it.
