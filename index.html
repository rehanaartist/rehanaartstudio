THE ARTIST

A life observed.
A feeling remembered.

Welcome to my little corner of the art world. I have always been drawn to the atmosphere of places, the stories held by ordinary moments, and the quiet emotions that are sometimes difficult to put into words.

Through my paintings, I try to preserve a little of that feeling. My hope is that when a piece finds its way into your home, it becomes more than something on a wall — it becomes something personal to you, too.

Rehana With warmth, and gratitude.
