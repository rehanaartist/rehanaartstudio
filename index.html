A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Every canvas
holds a piece of life.

I am Rehana, the artist behind Rehana Art Studio. I paint the places, moods, and small details that make ordinary moments feel extraordinary.

Through colour, light, and atmosphere, I hope to create paintings that feel familiar in a way that is difficult to explain — the kind of art that makes you pause and feel something of your own.
